# Spatial 3D Scenes & Depth Choreography (`fx/spatial/`)

Four deterministic `0BSD` spatial effects sharing one `Three.js` `WebGLRenderer` (plus pure Canvas 2D botanical depth composition).

| Demo ID | Role | Period | Hero | Highlights |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| `wireframe-portal` | Lead technique | 6.0 s | 2.80 s | 18 nested chamfered architectural portal frames, depth fog, focal title (`FX_CUSTOM_TEXT`) |
| `code-3d-extrude` | Lead technique | 6.0 s | 2.90 s | `ExtrudeGeometry` machined beveled code slab with syntax-highlighted face and active execution beam (`FX_CUSTOM_TEXT`) |
| `canopy-part-title` | Lead technique | 5.6 s | 2.75 s | 3-plane procedural veined foliage parting on springs with foreground bokeh and golden sunbeams (`FX_CUSTOM_TEXT`) |
| `device-flythrough` | Lead technique | 6.4 s | 3.10 s | Desktop, tablet and phone 3D hardware frames linked by a glowing Catmull-Rom ribbon and specular orb (`FX_CUSTOM_TEXT`) |
